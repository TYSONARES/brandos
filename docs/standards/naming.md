# Naming Standard

## Rule

Work related to naming must be explicit, reviewable, and consistent with `PROJECT_MANIFEST.md`.

## Checklist

- The change has a clear owner or decision source.
- Related indexes are updated.
- Terminology matches `docs/foundation/glossary.md`.
- AI-generated work is checked against repository files, not chat memory.

## Anti-Pattern

Do not leave durable decisions only in conversation history.
