# Approve Decision

## Trigger

A strategic choice needs to become official or be rejected.

## Owner

Brand Owner.

## Inputs

- Proposed Decision
- Rationale
- Consequences
- Affected Brand Profiles, Claims, Context Packs, or workflows

## Steps

1. Review proposal and evidence.
2. Identify affected product objects.
3. Accept, reject, or request changes.
4. Record status, owner, date, rationale, and consequences.
5. Update affected Claims, Brand Profiles, and Context Packs.

## Output

Accepted or rejected Decision.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Decision has status, owner, date, rationale, and consequences.
- Accepted Decisions are linked from affected objects.
- Rejected Decisions do not appear in active Context Packs.

## Failure States

- Consequences are unclear.
- Owner is not authorized to approve.
- Affected objects are not identified.
