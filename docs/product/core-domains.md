# Core Domains

## Domains

### Workspace

The container for a team, organization, or project using BrandOS.

### Brand Profile

The structured representation of a brand: positioning, audience, voice, taxonomy, proof, and constraints.

### Evidence

Source material used to justify or challenge brand claims. Evidence can include research notes, interviews,
strategy documents, campaign results, competitor observations, and approved stakeholder input.

### Decision

A durable strategic choice with status, owner, rationale, date, and consequences.

### Workflow

A repeatable sequence that transforms inputs into reviewed outputs.

### Context Pack

An AI-ready bundle of approved brand knowledge for a specific task, agent, channel, or product surface.

### Review

The approval layer that determines whether an object is draft, proposed, approved, deprecated, or rejected.

## Boundary Rule

If a concept cannot be assigned to one of these domains, it needs either a new decision record or a clearer product definition.
