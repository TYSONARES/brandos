# Source, Claim, Decision Model

## Source

A Source is evidence. It can support, challenge, or contextualize a Claim.

## Claim

A Claim is a specific statement BrandOS may reuse in brand profiles, reviews, and context packs.

## Decision

A Decision explains why a strategic choice became official.

## Relationship

1. Sources provide evidence.
2. Claims convert evidence into usable statements.
3. Decisions approve strategic direction and explain consequences.
4. Brand Profiles and Context Packs reuse approved Claims and Decisions.

## Approval Rule

A Claim should not become approved unless it has at least one linked Source or one linked accepted Decision.
