# Design Review Checklist

## Purpose

This checklist keeps BrandOS design review tied to repository truth and Product Core requirements.

## Required Checks

- The design maps to a documented Product Core object or workflow.
- The design uses approved or draft v0.3 tokens from `fixtures/tokens/`.
- The design references a component spec from `fixtures/components/` or proposes a new one.
- Object status, owner, date, and review state are visible where relevant.
- Empty, loading, error, blocked, expired, and deprecated states are considered.
- Primary and secondary actions are named clearly.
- Accessibility checklist requirements are satisfied.
- Copy does not introduce unsupported brand, product, legal, or customer claims.

## Outcome

Design review can result in approved, changes-needed, or rejected.

## Acceptance Rule

Approved design work must be traceable to Product Core docs, token files, component specs, and review notes.
